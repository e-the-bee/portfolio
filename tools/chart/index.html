Lanes: top lane kicks (drives --beat: big pulses), bottom lane hits (drives --hit: smaller accents). Marker height is its strength.

Mouse: click a lane to add a marker · drag a marker to move it · right-click a marker to delete · click the ruler or waveform to seek · scroll to pan · Ctrl+scroll to zoom.

Keys: Space play/pause · K drop a kick at the playhead · H drop a hit · 123 strength of the selected marker (and new ones) · , . nudge selected 10 ms (with Shift 1 ms) · Delete remove · ← → seek 1 s (Shift 5 s) · Ctrl+S save.

Workflow: the markers you open with are the analyzer's guess. Play at 0.5× with ticks on, delete the wrong ones, tap K/H where it misses, fix the timing with the nudge keys. Save writes src/beats.json and marks the song as hand-charted, so node tools/analyze-beats.mjs will not overwrite it.
